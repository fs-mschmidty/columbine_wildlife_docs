### Raptor Surveys

Raptor surveys were conducted in all proposed timber and fuels units using the Broadcast Acoustical Survey Method. Active raptor nests were found in the Bevel and Sun King timber units.

**Bevel - Red-tailed Hawk**

While the exact location of the Red-tailed Hawk was not found in the Bevel timber unit, we have narrowed the nest down to a small zone. Multiple adults were seen being very active and protective of this zone, producing many alarm calls. We suggest an 800 meter timing limitation for cutting from February 1 to July 15.

**Sun King - Cooper's Hawk**

An active Cooper's Hawk nest was found in the Sun King timber unit. At least two adults and one juvenile were seen in the area, vocalizing many alarm calls. The exact location of this nest has been verified. Our recommendation is 200 meter no cut buffer around the nest at all times of the year. In addition, we recommend an 800 meter timing limitation to cut from February 1 to July 15.

### North American Bat Monitoring

Annual NA Bat passive monitoring units were deployed at Lange Canyon, Relay Station, and two locations in Saul's Creek from July 15 to August 10.

At time of deployment in 2027, we will need to take out a T-post pounder to solidify the T-posts further into the ground.
